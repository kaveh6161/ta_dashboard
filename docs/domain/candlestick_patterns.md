# Candlestick Patterns

This document explains the candlestick patterns that may be identified in the TA Dashboard's technical analysis and AI interpretation.

## Candlestick Basics

A candlestick represents price movement over a specific timeframe and consists of:

- **Open**: Price at the beginning of the period
- **Close**: Price at the end of the period
- **High**: Highest price during the period
- **Low**: Lowest price during the period
- **Body**: Rectangle between open and close prices
- **Wick/Shadow**: Lines extending from the body to the high and low prices

**Bullish Candle**: Close price higher than open price (often green or white)
**Bearish Candle**: Close price lower than open price (often red or black)

## Single Candlestick Patterns

### Doji

A candlestick with a very small body where open and close prices are nearly equal.

**Appearance**: Cross, plus, or "T" shape
**Interpretation**: Indicates indecision and potential reversal when appearing after a strong trend

Variations:
- **Standard Doji**: Equal open and close with upper and lower shadows
- **Long-Legged Doji**: Equal open and close with long upper and lower shadows
- **Dragonfly Doji**: Equal open and close with a long lower shadow and no upper shadow
- **Gravestone Doji**: Equal open and close with a long upper shadow and no lower shadow

### Hammer/Hanging Man

**Appearance**: Small body at the top with a long lower shadow and little or no upper shadow
**Interpretation**:
- **Hammer**: Bullish reversal signal at the bottom of a downtrend
- **Hanging Man**: Bearish reversal signal at the top of an uptrend

### Shooting Star/Inverted Hammer

**Appearance**: Small body at the bottom with a long upper shadow and little or no lower shadow
**Interpretation**:
- **Shooting Star**: Bearish reversal signal at the top of an uptrend
- **Inverted Hammer**: Bullish reversal signal at the bottom of a downtrend

### Marubozu

**Appearance**: Long body with very small or no shadows
**Interpretation**:
- **Bullish Marubozu**: Strong buying pressure
- **Bearish Marubozu**: Strong selling pressure

### Spinning Top

**Appearance**: Small body with upper and lower shadows longer than the body
**Interpretation**: Indicates indecision between buyers and sellers

## Two-Candlestick Patterns

### Engulfing Pattern

**Appearance**: Second candle completely "engulfs" the body of the first candle
**Interpretation**:
- **Bullish Engulfing**: Bearish candle followed by a larger bullish candle (bullish reversal)
- **Bearish Engulfing**: Bullish candle followed by a larger bearish candle (bearish reversal)

### Harami

**Appearance**: Second candle's body is completely contained within the body of the first candle
**Interpretation**:
- **Bullish Harami**: Large bearish candle followed by a smaller bullish candle (bullish reversal)
- **Bearish Harami**: Large bullish candle followed by a smaller bearish candle (bearish reversal)

### Tweezer Tops/Bottoms

**Appearance**: Two candles with identical highs (tops) or identical lows (bottoms)
**Interpretation**:
- **Tweezer Top**: Bearish reversal signal at the top of an uptrend
- **Tweezer Bottom**: Bullish reversal signal at the bottom of a downtrend

## Three-Candlestick Patterns

### Morning Star/Evening Star

**Morning Star**:
**Appearance**: Bearish candle, followed by a small-bodied candle, followed by a bullish candle
**Interpretation**: Bullish reversal pattern at the bottom of a downtrend

**Evening Star**:
**Appearance**: Bullish candle, followed by a small-bodied candle, followed by a bearish candle
**Interpretation**: Bearish reversal pattern at the top of an uptrend

### Three White Soldiers/Three Black Crows

**Three White Soldiers**:
**Appearance**: Three consecutive bullish candles, each opening within the previous candle's body and closing higher
**Interpretation**: Strong bullish reversal pattern

**Three Black Crows**:
**Appearance**: Three consecutive bearish candles, each opening within the previous candle's body and closing lower
**Interpretation**: Strong bearish reversal pattern

### Three Inside Up/Down

**Three Inside Up**:
**Appearance**: Bearish candle, followed by a bullish candle within the first candle's range, followed by a bullish candle that closes above the first candle's open
**Interpretation**: Bullish reversal pattern

**Three Inside Down**:
**Appearance**: Bullish candle, followed by a bearish candle within the first candle's range, followed by a bearish candle that closes below the first candle's open
**Interpretation**: Bearish reversal pattern

## Multi-Candlestick Patterns

### Bullish/Bearish Abandoned Baby

**Appearance**: A doji with gaps on both sides (no overlap with the preceding or following candles)
**Interpretation**:
- **Bullish Abandoned Baby**: Bearish candle, followed by a lower doji, followed by a bullish candle (strong bullish reversal)
- **Bearish Abandoned Baby**: Bullish candle, followed by a higher doji, followed by a bearish candle (strong bearish reversal)

### Rising/Falling Windows (Gaps)

**Appearance**: Price gap between the high of one candle and the low of the next candle
**Interpretation**:
- **Rising Window**: Gap up, considered bullish
- **Falling Window**: Gap down, considered bearish

## Combination Patterns

### Bullish/Bearish Kicker

**Appearance**: A strong reversal pattern with a gap in the direction of the reversal
**Interpretation**: One of the strongest and most reliable reversal patterns

### Tasuki Gap

**Appearance**: Gap followed by a candle in the opposite direction that partially fills the gap
**Interpretation**:
- **Bullish Tasuki Gap**: Continuation of uptrend after a temporary pullback
- **Bearish Tasuki Gap**: Continuation of downtrend after a temporary rally

## Reliability Factors

The reliability of candlestick patterns increases when:

1. **Pattern occurs at a key support/resistance level**
2. **Pattern is accompanied by high volume**
3. **Pattern is confirmed by the following candle(s)**
4. **Pattern coincides with readings from other technical indicators**
5. **Pattern occurs after a strong trend**

The TA Dashboard's AI analysis evaluates these reliability factors when identifying candlestick patterns and provides confidence scores to help traders assess the strength of the signal.
