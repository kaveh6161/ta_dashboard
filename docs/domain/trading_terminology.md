# Trading Terminology

This document explains key trading terminology used throughout the TA Dashboard.

## Time-Based Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Timeframe** | The period represented by each candlestick (1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 1d, 1wk) |
| **Period** | The historical time range for analysis (1d, 5d, 1mo, 3mo, 6mo, 1y, 2y, 5y, max) |
| **Session** | Trading period within a day (pre-market, regular, after-hours) |
| **Overnight** | Price movement between market close and next day's open |

## Price Action Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Bull Market** | Upward price trend characterized by higher highs and higher lows |
| **Bear Market** | Downward price trend characterized by lower highs and lower lows |
| **Sideways/Range** | Price moving horizontally between support and resistance levels |
| **Breakout** | Price moving above resistance or below support with increased volume |
| **Retracement** | Temporary price movement against the prevailing trend |
| **Reversal** | Change in trend direction from bullish to bearish or vice versa |
| **Gap** | Area on a chart where no trading occurred between two price points |
| **Support** | Price level where buying pressure tends to overcome selling pressure |
| **Resistance** | Price level where selling pressure tends to overcome buying pressure |

## Chart Patterns

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Head and Shoulders** | Reversal pattern with three peaks (middle highest) |
| **Double Top/Bottom** | Reversal pattern with two peaks/troughs at similar levels |
| **Triangle** | Continuation pattern where price range narrows (ascending, descending, symmetrical) |
| **Flag/Pennant** | Short-term continuation pattern after strong price movement |
| **Cup and Handle** | Bullish continuation pattern resembling a cup with a handle |
| **Channel** | Price moving between parallel trendlines (ascending, descending, horizontal) |
| **Wedge** | Price pattern with converging trendlines (rising or falling) |

## Trading Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Entry Point** | Price level at which a trader opens a position |
| **Exit Point** | Price level at which a trader closes a position |
| **Stop Loss** | Predetermined price at which a trade is exited to limit losses |
| **Take Profit** | Predetermined price at which a trade is exited to secure gains |
| **Risk/Reward Ratio** | Ratio of potential profit to potential loss in a trade |
| **Position Sizing** | Determining how much capital to allocate to a single trade |
| **Market Order** | Order to buy/sell at the current market price |
| **Limit Order** | Order to buy/sell at a specified price or better |
| **Trailing Stop** | Stop loss that adjusts as price moves favorably |

## Analysis Approaches

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Technical Analysis** | Study of price action and volume to forecast future movements |
| **Fundamental Analysis** | Study of economic factors affecting a security's value |
| **Sentiment Analysis** | Assessment of market participants' attitudes toward a security |
| **Quantitative Analysis** | Mathematical and statistical techniques to analyze market data |
| **Algorithmic Trading** | Using computer algorithms to execute trades |

## Trading Strategies

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Trend Following** | Strategy that aims to capture gains by following the trend direction |
| **Mean Reversion** | Strategy based on the concept that prices will revert to their average |
| **Breakout Trading** | Entering trades when price breaks above resistance or below support |
| **Swing Trading** | Holding positions for days to weeks to capture price "swings" |
| **Day Trading** | Opening and closing positions within the same trading day |
| **Scalping** | Very short-term trading seeking small profits with high frequency |
| **Position Trading** | Long-term strategy holding positions for months or years |

## Risk Management Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Drawdown** | Decline from a peak to a trough in portfolio value |
| **Volatility** | Rate at which price increases or decreases for a security |
| **Diversification** | Spreading investments across different assets to reduce risk |
| **Correlation** | Statistical measure of how two securities move in relation to each other |
| **Beta** | Measure of a stock's volatility in relation to the overall market |
| **Alpha** | Excess return of an investment relative to a benchmark index |
| **Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE)** | Largest potential loss in a trade before closing |

## AI-Related Trading Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Confidence Score** | AI's certainty level in its analysis or prediction |
| **Pattern Recognition** | AI identification of chart patterns and formations |
| **Sentiment Score** | Numerical representation of positive/negative sentiment |
| **Signal Strength** | Measure of how strong a trading signal is |
| **False Positive** | Incorrect bullish signal generated by an indicator or algorithm |
| **False Negative** | Incorrect bearish signal generated by an indicator or algorithm |
| **Backtesting** | Testing a trading strategy on historical data |

## Volume Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Volume** | Number of shares or contracts traded in a given period |
| **Average Volume** | Mean trading volume over a specific timeframe |
| **Volume Profile** | Distribution of volume at different price levels |
| **Relative Volume** | Current volume compared to average volume |
| **Volume Spike** | Sudden significant increase in trading volume |
| **Accumulation** | Buying activity indicated by rising prices with increasing volume |
| **Distribution** | Selling activity indicated by falling prices with increasing volume |

These trading terms are commonly used throughout the TA Dashboard and in the AI-generated analyses. Understanding these terms is essential for interpreting the dashboard's outputs and making informed trading decisions.
